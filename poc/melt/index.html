Each melt point blurs the ink around it and re-thresholds it, so strokes swell, corners round off and letters bleed into each other. Passes is how many blur-and-rethreshold rounds; threshold is the ink level — low bleeds, high erodes.

Your file never leaves this browser. Nothing is uploaded; the melt runs on a canvas here, and your points are kept in local storage.

Export traces the melted mask at twice the working resolution and writes it as one evenodd path in the original SVG's coordinate space.
